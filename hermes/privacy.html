Privacy & Data-Use Disclosure

Hermes Personal Automation — Google account data

1. Who we are

This application ("Hermes") is a personal automation agent operated by Carl Eshen ("the operator") on the operator's own computer. It serves exactly one user: the operator. There is no other customer, user base, or data collection from any other party. Contact: the operator, via the support email shown on the OAuth consent screen.

2. What Google account data is accessed

With the operator's explicit OAuth consent, the application accesses the following scopes, all restricted to the single consenting Google account:

  • Gmail — gmail.readonly, gmail.send, gmail.modify (reading email; sending or labelling messages only when the operator explicitly requests it);
  • Google Calendar — listing and managing calendar events;
  • Google Drive — accessing files the operator asks about;
  • Google Sheets and Google Docs — reading and editing documents at the operator's request;
  • Google Contacts — contacts.readonly (resolving names mentioned in tasks).

3. How the data is processed

Data is fetched from Google's APIs at the moment a task is requested and is processed for that task only. Typical processing includes summarising unread email, listing upcoming calendar events, and drafting replies or documents. The operator reviews and approves every action before the application sends, modifies or deletes anything.

4. Third-party AI providers

To interpret requests and produce summaries and drafts, the application sends task content — which may include snippets of email text, event details, or document excerpts — to large language model (AI) providers used by the Hermes software. At the time of writing these are OpenRouter (API gateway) and the model provider it routes to (e.g. Z.ai). Such providers receive only the text needed for the task, do not use it to advertise to anyone, and process it under their own published terms. No Google account passwords, tokens, or full mailboxes are sent to AI providers — only the specific content required for the current task. If a spoken (voice) summary is requested, the text to be spoken may also be sent to a text-to-speech service (currently Microsoft's Edge TTS).

5. Storage and retention

  • OAuth tokens (the credential that lets the application connect) are stored locally on the operator's own computer only, in the Hermes application's local data folder. They are never uploaded to any server operated by the operator.
  • Email and document content is processed transiently for each task; generated summaries may be written to the operator's local machine as part of the requested output. There is no central database and no long-term archive of Google data.

6. Sharing

Google account data is shared with no one except: (a) Google's own APIs (source of the data), and (b) the AI providers described in §4, strictly as needed to complete a requested task. Data is never sold, rented, or used to train models by the operator, and no advertising identifiers are collected.

7. Revocation

The operator can revoke this application's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revocation immediately disables all data access.
